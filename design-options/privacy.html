Privacy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Tylilo, LLC (“Tylilo”) runs tylilo.com. This page says what the site actually does with information. It is short on purpose.

Contact form

If you write to us, the form asks for your name, email address, and message. We email those three things to ourselves so we can read them and reply. The site does not show our mailbox address. The site does not sell that information, and it does not send it to advertisers or other companies.

We do not keep a database of messages. Your words sit in a temporary server session only long enough to show a thank-you or an error, and then that copy is removed.

Cookies

The homepage sets one cookie named tylilo. It is a session cookie: it lasts until you close the browser. It is HttpOnly, so page scripts cannot read it, and SameSite is Lax. When you visit over HTTPS, it is also marked Secure.

That cookie exists so the contact form can tell a real visit from a forged or repeated one. It is not a tracking cookie. The site does not set advertising cookies.

What we don’t add

The site code does not include analytics, ads, or third-party tracking scripts. The contact form loads Cloudflare Turnstile only to tell people from bots. Fonts are files on this site, not a font service.
