Design options

Three local previews for tylilo.com. The live site is unchanged: these pages are not linked from it, and opening one does not change what tylilo.com serves.

From the site root, run python3 -m http.server 8765 and open /design-options/. Add ?theme=dark or ?theme=light to any preview, or use the theme switch on the page. Arcade cover art is the current artwork, unchanged.
