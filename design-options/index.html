Design options

Six local previews for tylilo.com. A, B, and C are the first set. D, E, and F are a more professional company site, kept in the same harness. The live site is unchanged: these pages are not linked from it, and opening one does not change what tylilo.com serves.

From the site root, run python3 -m http.server 8765 and open /design-options/. Add ?d=a through ?d=f, and ?theme=dark or ?theme=light, or use the theme switch on the page. Arcade cover art is the current artwork, unchanged.
